# APR7 — Pricing, Subscription & Revenue Operations

**APQC CX Process Area:** Pricing, Subscription & Revenue Operations  
**CX Capability Area:** Billing, Subscription, Entitlements  
**Bootcamp:** **Architecting Recurring Revenue Enterprises**

## Purpose

Define the capability and architecture boundary for Pricing, Subscription & Revenue Operations within the Autonomous CX enterprise.

## Bootcamp Focus

**Architecting Recurring Revenue Enterprises** connects monetization, subscription lifecycle and revenue operations.

- Pricing and monetization models
- Subscription lifecycle management
- Billing and invoicing
- Entitlements and usage
- Renewal, expansion and recurring revenue orchestration

## Capability Scope

Billing, Subscription, Entitlements

## Example Platforms

SAP BRIM, Zuora, Chargebee

> Platform examples are illustrative. The capability is platform-neutral and may be implemented through one or more products, services, data platforms, or custom capabilities.

## Architecture Concerns

- Monetization capability and customer lifecycle alignment
- Product, pricing, subscription, billing and entitlement data
- Subscription and revenue architecture
- Usage and event-driven charging
- API, event, and integration architecture
- AI-assisted pricing and renewal opportunities
- Customer billing experience
- Security, identity, privacy, and financial controls
- Recurring revenue, churn, renewal, and value realization

## Autonomy Pattern

Observe → Understand → Decide → Recommend → Act → Verify → Learn
