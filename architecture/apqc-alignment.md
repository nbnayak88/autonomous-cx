# APQC Alignment

This document provides the governance point for aligning Autonomous CX capabilities and processes with APQC process taxonomy.

The CX architecture remains capability-led and does not force a one-to-one relationship between an APQC process and a software module.

| Code | CX Process Area | CX Capability Area |
|---|---|---|
| ACM1 | Customer Management Strategy | Customer 360, Customer Strategy, Master Data |
| AMK2 | Marketing & Demand Generation | Campaigns, Personalization, Lead Generation |
| ASL3 | Sales Management | Opportunity Management, CPQ, Pipeline, Revenue |
| AC04 | Commerce & Digital Channels | B2B/B2C Commerce, Marketplace, Omnichannel |
| ACS5 | Customer Service & Support | Ticketing, Contact Center, Field Service |
| ACX6 | Customer Experience & Loyalty | Experience Management, Loyalty, Voice of Customer |
| APR7 | Pricing, Subscription & Revenue Operations | Billing, Subscription, Entitlements |
| AOF8 | Order Fulfillment & Customer Operations | Order Management, Fulfillment, Returns |
| ADI9 | Customer Data & Intelligence | CDP, Analytics, Segmentation, AI Insights |
| AEX0 | Digital Experience & Self-Service | Portals, Mobile, Communities |
| AAI1 | AI, Automation & Conversational CX | AI Agents, Bots, Recommendations |
| AIG2 | CX Integration & Enterprise Architecture | APIs, Middleware, Event Mesh, Identity |

Detailed mappings should be maintained at process, sub-process, and activity level as the model matures.
